# ADR 0093: Judge-path polish, 2026-11-20 (S13): Arabic claims surfaces, an honest Live toggle, recorded-example labels, the worker's off-shift line

- **Status:** Accepted by the architect on the owner's instruction (the owner's brief of 2026-11-20: English and Arabic must be complete, correct and polished; the other four languages stay as shipped); he can overrule.
- **Date:** 2026-11-20
- **Deciders:** Ali (owner, by brief); the implementer (Claude)
- **Supersedes / superseded by:** none. Amends [ADR 0083](0083-tier-2-english-only-story-copy.md) decision 1 for Arabic only, and resolves [ADR 0092](0092-deferrals-after-the-real-earth-landing.md) items 5 (for Arabic) and 10. Nothing is deleted.
- **Evidence:** QA explorers' findings of 2026-11-20 against the deployed demo; `frontend/scripts/i18n-sync.mjs`; `frontend/src/i18n/claims-english.tsx`; `frontend/src/features/today/describe.ts`; `frontend/src/features/supervisor/crew-model.ts`; `frontend/src/components/shell/DemoModeSwitch.tsx`; `frontend/src/features/judge/governance/GovernanceLayout.tsx`; `frontend/data/recorded/governance-run.json` (`source: "msw-mock"`).

## Context
Judges click through the deployed demo. Two QA explorers found defects on that path. The ones that need a decision, not just a fix, are recorded here.

## Decisions
1. **Arabic claims surfaces are translated** (amends ADR 0083 decision 1, Arabic only). ADR 0083 kept the claims surfaces English because "a machine translation of a verdict is an unverified claim". The owner ruled that Arabic must be complete. The guard that carried the old rule is replaced, for Arabic, by a rule a machine can check: **every number of the English sentence is in the Arabic sentence, the same number of times** (`numberTokens` in `i18n-sync.mjs`; `i18n:check` fails otherwise), on top of the existing placeholder, tag and Western-digit rules. 654 keys were drafted (`judge.scoreboard.`, `judge.frontier.`, `judge.proof.`, `judge.day.`, `judge.try.`, `judge.governance.`, `judge.landing.story.`, `judge.tour.steps.`, `states.proof.`). They are `machine-draft` in the manifest, and the "Machine translation, not yet reviewed by a native speaker" notice stays wherever machine text shows. **What the numbers rule does not check:** the meaning, the direction word of a verdict ("fewer", "met"), or grammar. Those need a native reviewer, and that need is the ADR 0083 revisit trigger, now open for Arabic. The other four languages keep the English claims surfaces (`CLAIMS_TRANSLATED_LOCALES = ["ar"]`: one list in `i18n-sync.mjs`, one in `claims-english.tsx`, a test keeps them equal).
2. **Claims blocks follow the page language where the claims are translated.** `ClaimsEnglish`, `claimsAttrs` and `claimsLocale` give `lang=ar dir=rtl` and Arabic dates on `/ar`, and `lang=en dir=ltr` and English formats in the other languages. Text that is English by nature (a legal clause, a sentence a scoring run wrote, shown in "For auditors") keeps its own `lang=en dir=ltr`.
3. **"Replay" in Arabic is "إعادة عرض"** (a replay of a recording), not "إعادة تشغيل" (restart). It is used in the Replay/Live toggle and in the Replay stamp.
4. **The Live option is disabled where the deployment answers with a replay** (it changes what the switch shows, not the rule of [ADR 0051](0051-api-v1-surface-for-the-core-demo-path.md) decision 4: the API's `mode` stamp, never the switch, says what the data is). Live is not pressable, never shown as pressed, and a line says "Live feeds are not connected in this demo". A stored Live choice is put back to Replay. A deployment that answers `live` shows two normal buttons: the capability and its code path stay. The tour no longer tells judges to switch to Live.
5. **A recording made against the mock is labelled "Recorded example", and its placeholder ids are not printed.** The governance page drew `mock-req-1` and `mock-0` and "Recorded against the demo mock" before any call. The real demo API could not be used for the first paint: a call writes to the shared, append-only audit log ([ADR 0054](0054-governance-refusal-and-hash-chained-audit.md), [0066](0066-demo-reset-is-a-new-epoch-audit-stays-append-only.md)), and the page is static, so a first-paint call would run for every visitor. So the recording stays, its ids become "Recorded example", the notice says "A recorded example, not a live call. Press the button to make a real call to the demo API.", and the live 403 is still one press away and prints its own ids. Nothing says a live call happened when it did not.
6. **The worker screen says when the crew is off shift** ([ADR 0046](0046-warning-level-policy.md), [0057](0057-plans-row-baselines-and-crew-fixture.md), [0034](0034-demo-strategy.md)). At the same replay time (about 13:57) the worker screen showed "Work 20 min, rest 40 min" under Orange while the crew pages said "off shift now, back at 15:00". Two rules, both intended: the worker's action line is the **site's** rule for the hour (`current_hour.action_codes`, ADR 0046), and the crew pages read the **crew's** shift blocks (`CrewTimeline.shift`, `crew-model.ts`: "off shift" is no block covering now). The worker screen already had the same blocks and ignored the gap. It now adds one plain line under the action when no block covers now: "Your crew is off shift now, back at 15:00. The line above is the site rule for anyone who works before then." No backend policy or model behaviour changed, and the action line is unchanged.
7. **Layout.** Fixed bottom bars publish their height; the page pads its end with it and `html` gets `scroll-padding-bottom` from the tallest bar, so a scrolled-to or Tab-focused control stops above the bar (WCAG 2.2 Focus Not Obscured). The desk menu wraps onto a second row on a phone instead of scrolling sideways. A clock with no data is invisible, not "--:--". The Stop work bar says "<crew> is off shift now. You can still stop work." when the crew is off shift; the button is unchanged.
8. **The tour has a "One day" stop** (resolves ADR 0092 item 10), between the crew lead's screen and Try a plan.

## Rejected options
- Translate the claims surfaces in all five languages: the owner asked for Arabic; the other four stay as shipped and secondary.
- Re-record the governance run against the live API: it would write an audit row on the shared deployment.
- A z-index change or a taller fixed padding for the dock: neither fixes keyboard focus, which the browser does not scroll when a control is inside the viewport but under the bar.

## Consequences
- **Cost of being wrong:** an Arabic verdict whose wording is off but whose numbers are right passes the gate. Mitigation: the notice, the manifest status, and the open need for a native reviewer.
- The Live toggle is unavailable on every deployment until one answers `live`; a judge sees the reason.
- On first paint the governance page shows "Recorded example" where it showed ids; a reader who wants real ids presses the button.

## Revisit trigger
A native Arabic reviewer is found (move the Arabic keys to `reviewed`); a deployment with a live feed (the Live option returns by itself); the governance page can read the real log without a write on first paint.
