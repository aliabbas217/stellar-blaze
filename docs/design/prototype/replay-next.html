What's next

Seven next steps, grouped by how far each one is from real. Each says what exists today and what would trigger it. Nothing on this page is claimed as done.

  • ExistsThe part we can show is built and tested. The next step is configuration, not invention.
  • DesignedSpecified in a decision record. Building it is ours to do, and it is not built.
  • Needs pilotCannot move without a partner: a site, devices or a regulator.

Designed

Regulator view

Designed
Next step
A view for the regulator of hours by site and zone: High-zone hours, plans signed, stops. Small groups are hidden.
Exists now
The aggregation rules: groups under 10 (health) or 5 (exposure) are hidden, and every employer sees only its own crews.
Trigger
Interest from the Abu Dhabi regulator and a data-sharing agreement. The workplace code of practice already asks sites to keep incident records.
