Why every number is a range

The temperature source has no humidity, and heat stress depends strongly on humidity. So each scenario runs twice, at the two ends of what humidity could do.

Lower end: specific humidity held fixed

The air keeps the moisture it had, so relative humidity falls as it warms. The observed dew point stays; only temperature rises.

Upper end: relative humidity held fixed

The air holds more moisture as it warms, so specific humidity rises about 6 to 7 % for each °C of warming.

Near a warming sea the truth is plausibly between the two ends, so the result is shown as a range and never as one number. The end that drives each counter: ⟨bracket_driver⟩.
