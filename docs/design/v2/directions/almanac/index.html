1 · The problem

The midday ban covers about one in ten of the hours too hot for heavy work

From 15 June to 15 September the law stops outdoor work from 12:30 to 15:00. The heat does not keep to that window. Mornings pass the limit by nine, and on many evenings it is still above the line at eight.

A crew needs to know which hours, not which months. That is the forecast we give them the evening before.
