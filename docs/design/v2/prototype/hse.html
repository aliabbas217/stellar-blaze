How data gets here

The forecast refreshes on a schedule and sets the heat for every hour. Supervisors sign a plan for each crew. This page counts hours, plans and stops for the site and week. It never receives names or symptoms.

What you cannot see here

What if someone asks for one worker?
  • Worker names. Counts never carry a name. The roster's name column is dropped in the supervisor's browser.
  • Symptoms. "I feel unwell" goes to a first-aider and never reaches this page.
  • Any worker's personal risk. It is worked out on the worker's phone and stays there.
