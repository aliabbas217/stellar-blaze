Where the rise comes from

IPCC AR6 Atlas, Arabian Peninsula region including sea (land only reported next to it). Global warming levels, not calendar years. Model median rise between today's level and the chosen one.

Why each result is a range

The rise has no humidity in it. Each scenario runs twice: specific humidity held (the air keeps its moisture) and relative humidity held (about 6 to 7 % more moisture per °C). Truth near a warming sea is plausibly between.
