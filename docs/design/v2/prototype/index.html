      Chapter 1 of 6 · The problem

      The midday break stops 279 of the 1,624 working hours too hot for heavy work

      From 15 June to 15 September the law stops outdoor work from 12:30 to 15:00. The heat does not keep to that window: mornings pass the limit by nine, and evenings are still above it at six.

      A crew needs to know which hours, not which months. That is the forecast we give them the evening before.
