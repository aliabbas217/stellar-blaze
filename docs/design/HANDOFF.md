## Decisions Ali must make (raised by this design phase)

1. **Approve the direction as revised (R1-R6)**, or keep the candidate as is. Recommendation: approve.
2. **Where does "I feel unwell" send?** Options: (a) a site first-aider role only (recommended; satisfies G9),
   (b) no send at all, tel: links only (simplest, zero privacy risk), (c) supervisor (rejected: violates G9).
3. **Worker light-by-default (R2).** Recommendation: yes, with the toggle.
4. **Two added demo screens** (Governance refusal, Break things) were not in the brief but are on the never-cut
   lists. Recommendation: keep.
5. **F1 Task 8 test change:** "submit disabled until consent ticked" conflicts with the web-interface guideline
   "don't pre-disable submit". Recommendation: keep submit enabled and show the inline consent error.
